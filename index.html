01 / The philosophy

It's never
just about
getting there.

A gold tank against a block wall. A black Chevy in the shade of a cedar. A yellow Riviera on a farm road with the clouds sitting on the hills. The machines were already the journal. They just needed a name on the masthead.

Mixture Motor is that name, taken out of the drawer. Not a catalog. A place to look at the cars and the bike a little longer than a camera roll allows, and to leave room for whatever comes next.

An appreciation of all things motor.

MXM / 001 · Two wheels

The gold tank.

White seat, gold flanks, a pipe that could cut a finger. Parked like it has already been somewhere.

This is the picture the name was waiting for. Not a show stand. A block wall, a little gravel, a bike that has been thought about. The tank is the whole argument: gold where a catalog would have put black, white where a catalog would have put a logo.

A journal can start here and never run out. The next photograph is the same bike from the other side, or the same bike dirty. The page is ready either way.

MXM / 002 · Four wheels

Black, in the shade.

An early Chevrolet, the kind of black that holds a whole afternoon in the paint.

The photograph is a little warm, a little soft, which is correct. Cars from this era should not be lit like appliances. The grille is a face. The bumper is jewelry. The trees behind it are doing the work of a studio.

Leave the caption short. Anyone who knows the car will fill in the year. Anyone who doesn't will still stop.

← Back to the mix
MXM / 003 · Four wheels

The red spear.

A black Edsel, white roof, one red stripe doing all the talking. Palms for a backdrop, because California insists.

The side of this car is a drawing. The cove, the chrome line, the little kick of the fin. It is the sort of machine people apologize for liking, which is usually a sign they should like it more.

Mixture Motor does not apologize. If it has an engine and a story, it belongs in the mix. This one has both, and a roofline worth the whole roll of film.

MXM / 004 · The road

Yellow, low.

A Riviera sitting on the white line like it paid for the road.

Gray sky, brown field, a fence that goes nowhere in particular. The car is the only saturated thing in the county, which is how a Riviera prefers it. Wires in the wheels. A deck lid with a little crest and no need to explain itself.

Open the trunk on another day and the story changes: amplifiers, a bottle, stickers from people who have already decided what this car is. Both pictures are true. The journal can hold both when you want it to. For now, the road is enough.

MXM / 005 · The workshop

The working one.

A Chevrolet pickup in a color somewhere between sand and a good suit. Stepside. Whitewalls. Still a truck.

Not everything in the mix has to lean into a corner. This one hauls, or could, which is the more interesting claim. The bed is empty on purpose. An empty bed is a promise.

Put it next to the cafe racer and you have the whole range of the name: one machine for a Saturday morning, one for everything else.

MXM / 006 · The open road

Take the long way.

Because sometimes the best part of owning a machine is simply finding somewhere to take it.

Stevens Creek, the leaves already decided, the road still wet in the shade. This is the newest machine in the set and it earns its place the same way the others do: it went somewhere worth a photograph.

A journal that only looked backward would be a museum. Mixture Motor keeps a page for the car that is still accumulating miles, and for the next road that looks like this one.
